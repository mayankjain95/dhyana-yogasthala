FAQs

Your questions, answered

Who can participate?

Women who are more than three months pregnant.

What will I be learning?

Not all yoga postures are suitable during pregnancy, so this is not a regular yoga class. You will learn only gentle practices designed for this period, taught step by step, so you can practise with confidence. It is something special, made for you and your baby.

Is there any prerequisite? Do I need prior experience?

No. There are no prerequisites and no prior yoga experience is needed.

How long is the programme?

Three days, two hours each, so six hours in total. Once you know the practice, doing it on your own takes only about 30 minutes. The longer workshop is there to make sure you learn it correctly.

Is it in person or online?

You can choose. In person is preferred. If you are not in Sonepat, for any reason, you can choose online.

Where is it held?

At Dhyana Yogasthala, 812, Sector 15, Sonepat, Haryana 131001.

What is the fee?

₹6,500, which includes the workshop as well as the review.

What should I bring?

Just a yoga mat. If you don’t have one, don’t worry, mats are available for you to use. Wear comfortable clothing.

Do I need to be on an empty stomach?

Please keep a gap of two hours before the class. If you have any health concern about maintaining this gap, consult the teacher and she will guide you accordingly.

I won’t be able to practise yoga for two hours.

Don’t worry, you won’t be practising for the whole two hours. Each session includes a demonstration, a review of what you learned and a Q&A, with enough breaks for you to settle down.

I’m scared something may happen.

The practices are chosen specifically for pregnancy and taught step by step. The batch is small, about two to three women, so you receive personal attention. You don’t need to worry about getting it wrong.

What if I forget the practice, or can’t do it properly later?

Stay in personal touch with the teacher. You will be guided within 24 hours on what to do if you have any doubts. The review after three to four weeks will settle any remaining queries.

What is the cancellation policy?

If you have registered, we are sure you want to be there. If you can’t make it, please let the teacher know one day in advance. If the reason is urgent and genuine, your place will be rescheduled and you can join the next programme.
