FAQs

Your questions, answered

Who can attend this workshop?

Anyone aged 14 and above who has back or cervical pain, or who wants to strengthen their spine.

Do I need any prior yoga experience?

No. The practice is designed to be accessible to beginners.

I already have cervical spondylosis or back pain. Can I join?

Please write to the teacher separately so she can confirm. If you are going through an acute or severe episode, consult your doctor before participating.

How much time does the practice take?

The practice takes approximately 10 minutes.

What will I learn in the workshop?

You will learn the 10-minute practice, including breathing and postures, and how to approach it correctly. You can also ask your questions in the live Q&A with a Classical Hatha Yoga teacher trained at Sadhguru Gurukulam.

Is the session live, or will I get a recorded video?

The workshop is conducted live online.

What if I have questions after the workshop?

You will be added to a WhatsApp group where you can ask your questions, and you will receive a response within 24 hours. You also get free participation for the next 3 months in upcoming cervical and back pain workshops as a post-programme support, so you can revisit the practice and clear any doubts.

Who will conduct the workshop?

Shruti Jain, a Classical Hatha Yoga teacher with over 2,000 hours of training under Sadhguru Gurukulam, will guide the session.

What is the fee?

The workshop fee is ₹399 per participant.

What are the dates and timings?

  • 14 November 2026 (Saturday): 11:00 AM–12:00 PM or 7:30–8:30 PM
How do I attend the workshop?

The workshop is conducted online. Once your registration is confirmed, you will receive the joining details.

Is pre-registration mandatory?

Yes. Pre-registration is mandatory so that the teacher can give personal attention to every participant.
