✦ HOLISTIC DEVELOPMENT

Four Pillars of Children’s Yoga

Classical Hatha Yoga is not physical gymnastics; it is a profound holistic science tailored specifically for growing bodies and blooming minds between 7 and 13 years of age.

01

Memory & Concentration

Balancing asanas and simple breath awareness stimulate neural plasticity and harmonize both brain hemispheres. Children naturally experience enhanced concentration during study hours, superior memory retention, and a quiet, uncluttered mind.

02

Spinal Strength & Posture

Heavy school backpacks, prolonged sitting in classrooms, and screen usage cause habitual slouching. Classical practices stretch and align the 24 vertebrae of the spine, cultivating strong spinal health, neuromuscular symmetry, and effortless physical poise.

03

Energy Levels & Immunity

Gentle, rhythmic movement increases oxygen uptake and vital lung capacity. It activates the lymphatic and endocrine systems, fortifying the child’s natural immunity against seasonal ailments while providing clean, sustained energy throughout the week.

04

Emotional Balance & Joy

Modern children often grapple with peer pressure, academic anxiety, and hyperactive restlessness. This class provides a peaceful, non-competitive space for them to ground themselves, channel their vitality constructively, and cultivate joyful inner resilience.

✦ COMPLETE HOLISTIC CURRICULUM

What Your Child Will Learn

Taught with patience, warmth, and joy in small batches of only 5 students, each child learns classical practices step-by-step so they can practice safely and independently.

Module 1 · Alignment & Joints

Upa-Yoga & Spinal Activation

Engaging, dynamic preparatory practices that activate the joints, loosen physical stiffness, and develop flexibility. Children learn to listen to their bodies and build a tall, healthy spine.

Module 2 · Classical Postures

Asanas for Balance & Stability

Age-appropriate classical hatha yoga postures that cultivate physical strength, balance, and spatial awareness. The instructor provides real-time posture adjustments over the live Zoom feed.

Module 3 · Breath & Centering

Conscious Breath Awareness & Daily Calm

Simple, sacred breathing techniques that quiet mental chatter and bring an immediate sense of peace. Children learn practical tools they can use before exams or bedtime to release tension and feel joyful.
