"In yoga, if there is diabetes... diabetes is not taken lightly as a disease." — Sadhguru

In yoga, diabetes is seen as a sign of a deeper disturbance in the body's fundamental structure — not something to be treated lightly. This disturbance is unique to every individual, so it has to be addressed on a personal level. Whatever the ailment, yoga's approach stays the same: balance and energize the body's energy system. The underlying premise is that when this energy is fully balanced and flowing, chronic disease — physical or mental — cannot take hold. So diabetes resolves not because the disease itself is being "treated," but because the disturbance behind it is. With consistent personal practice, Sadhguru says, people can move toward freedom from chronic ailments altogether.
