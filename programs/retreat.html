About the Space

A natural mud space, away from the noise of the city, built entirely of mud in a village amidst the greenery of Haryana.

The workshop hall and the huts are made of mud, keeping you cool in summers and warm in winters.

The space is blessed with Sadhguru Sannidhi and Devi Yantra – powerful consecrated objects that energize the space in a profound way, allowing you to experience the presence of Devi & Sadhguru more powerfully.

Is This Retreat for You?

This retreat may be for you if one of these rings true:

  • You've achieved much of what you once wanted. Before moving on to the next goal, you wonder if there is something deeper and more lasting.
  • You sense there may be a way of living with less effort, more open-heartedness, and greater aliveness.
  • You find yourself at a crossroads—in your work, your relationships, or within yourself.
  • You've become skilled at arranging the outer world, yet you're beginning to wonder whether learning to look inward is equally worth exploring.
  • Or perhaps you simply value setting aside a weekend for yourself—to learn, reflect, spend time in nature, and enjoy good company.
