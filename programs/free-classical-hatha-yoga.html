✦ WHAT YOU WILL EXPERIENCE

The 75-Minute Experiential Curriculum

A step-by-step classical sequence suitable for all age groups and physical capabilities.

Module 1 · Physical Alignment

Joint Lubrication & Upa-Yoga

Simple yet profound physical movements that loosen the joints, exercise the spine in all directions, and dissolve accumulated physical tension.

Module 2 · Pranic Equilibrium

Nadi Shuddhi Pranayama

The foundational breathwork practice balancing the solar (pingala) and lunar (ida) channels, purifying energy pathways and steadying the mind.

Module 3 · Sound Resonance

Nada Yoga & Sacred Chanting

Using the elemental vibration of sound (AUM) to bring cellular harmony, relax muscle fibers, and clear mental turbulence.

Module 4 · Inward Absorption

Guided Meditation (Isha Kriya)

A gentle, guided inward journey to transcend surface-level thought patterns and taste an effortless state of presence and deep relaxation.
