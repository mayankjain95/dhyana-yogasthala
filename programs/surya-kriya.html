FAQs

Your questions, answered

What is Surya Kriya?

Surya Kriya is a powerful 21-step yogic process to activate your inner energy, bring balance and experience greater well-being.

Is Surya Kriya the same as Surya Namaskar?

No. Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutation) evolved from Surya Kriya as a more aerobic practice. Surya Kriya is a deeper and more spiritual process, and far more powerful in its impact on the human system.

Who can attend?

Anyone aged 14 and above can attend.

Do I need any prior yoga experience?

No prior experience is needed.

How long is the program?

The program runs for three days, two hours each day.

Do I need to practise for 2 hours every day?

No. The learning sessions are two hours each. Once you have learned the practice, you only need to practise for 30–40 minutes every day.

I won’t be able to practise yoga for two hours.

Don’t worry, you won’t be practising for the whole two hours. Each session includes a demonstration, a review of what you learned and a Q&A, with enough breaks for you to settle down.

Do I need to be on an empty stomach?

Please keep a gap of four hours after a full meal before the class. You can have a light snack until 2.5 hours before the program starts. If you have any health concern about maintaining this gap, consult the teacher and she will guide you accordingly.

I’m scared something may happen.

The practices are taught step by step, and the batch is small, so you receive personal attention. You don’t need to worry about getting it wrong.

What if I forget the practice, or can’t do it properly later?

Stay in personal touch with the teacher. You will be guided within 24 hours on what to do if you have any doubts, and the review after three to four weeks will settle any remaining queries.

Can it help with PCOS, PCOD, thyroid or menopause-related symptoms?

The practice has helped thousands of people looking for hormonal balance. Please note that it is not a substitute for medical treatment, so continue your treatment.

I have a medical condition. Can I still join?

Please write to the teacher before registering, and consult your doctor as well.
