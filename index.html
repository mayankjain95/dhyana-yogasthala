Meet Your Teacher

Shruti Jain

Chartered Accountant · Classical Hatha Yoga Teacher

After qualifying as a Chartered Accountant in 2016 and spending nearly five years with organisations such as PricewaterhouseCoopers and MaxLife Insurance, Shruti chose to follow a deeper calling.

She underwent intensive Classical Hatha Yoga teacher training at Sadhguru Gurukulam, Isha Yoga Center, Coimbatore, completing more than 2,000 hours of training.

She had the opportunity to touch 1000+ lives from young children to officers of the Indian Army, from homemakers to CEOs of leading organisations.

Today, she offers guided Classical Hatha Yoga programs for adults, children and expectant mothers, both in person in Sonipat and online.

Ancient Science

What Is Classical
Hatha Yoga?

Classical Hatha Yoga is a meticulous ancient science, not exercise, not stretching, but a precise technology for aligning the body, mind and energy with the existence. It offers a holistic path to health, joy and inner well-being.

At Dhyana Yogasthala, every practice is offered exactly as transmitted by Sadhguru through the Sadhguru Gurukulam — one of the most rigorous yoga teacher training programmes in the world. Not as fitness choreography, but as a sacred process of transformation.

Questions

FAQs

Who will teach the programs?

The programs are led by Shruti Jain, a Classical Hatha Yoga teacher trained at Sadhguru Gurukulam.

Are the programs conducted online or in person?

Dhyana Yogasthala offers in-person programs in Sonipat and selected live online programs. The format is specified on each program page.

Who can attend?

Anyone aged 14 and above can attend. The Children’s program is for ages 7–13.

Do I need any prior yoga experience?

No prior experience is needed.

I have a medical condition. Can I still join?

Please write to the teacher before registering and consult your doctor as well, if you aren’t sure.

Are the programs suitable during pregnancy?

The Prenatal Yoga Program is designed specifically for expectant mothers. You can register for that program.

How big is the batch?

2–3 people in the in-person programs (Surya Kriya and Prenatal Yoga). 8–10 children in the Children’s program.

I wanted a daily yoga class. After attending the workshop, I feel I may forget the practice, and can’t do it properly later?

Workshop has been designed in a way that takes care of this aspect. Stay in personal touch with the teacher. You will be guided within 24 hours on what to do if you have any doubts. The review after three to four weeks will settle any remaining queries.

It has worked for thousands of people. Don't worry :)

How can I register?

Select your preferred program and follow the registration instructions. You can also contact the teacher on WhatsApp for assistance.

What if the next batch dates haven't been announced?

You can enquire about the upcoming batch and request to be notified when dates are confirmed.

What is the cancellation policy?

If you have registered, we are sure you want to be there. If you can’t make it, please let the teacher know one day in advance. If the reason is urgent and genuine, your place will be rescheduled and you can join the next program.
