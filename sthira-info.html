The Teacher

These are not park yoga exercises.

These are time-tested tools that have survived for thousands of years — refined, preserved, and now offered by Sadhguru for the demands of modern life.

Shruti Jain qualified as a Chartered Accountant in 2016 and went on to work in the corporate world with organisations such as PricewaterhouseCoopers and MaxLife Insurance. She understands, from the inside, what it means to work under pressure, to push through exhaustion, and to perform when it counts.

Some longing within her led her to the Isha Yoga Center, Coimbatore — where she underwent an intensive 21-week Classical Hatha Yoga teacher training and spent more than two years immersed in these practices. She brings both the precision of a CA and the depth of a committed yogi to every class she offers.

Common Questions

FAQs

Do I need any prior yoga experience? +
No — Sthira is designed for complete beginners. No flexibility required, no special equipment, no prior experience. Just show up and follow along.
How many sessions are there per week? +
Three sessions per week — either Monday, Wednesday, Friday (MWF) or Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday (TTS). Each session is 35 minutes, available in a morning batch (6:00–6:35 AM) and an evening batch (8:30–9:05 PM).
What if I miss a session? +
If you miss the morning session, you can attend the evening session of the same day. For genuine reasons where you are unable to attend a session or sessions, please speak to Shruti directly — she will work with you to help you catch up with the missed sessions.
Will this take too much time from my studies? +
Each session is just 35 minutes, three times a week. Most students find they actually study better on days they practise — the time investment consistently returns more focus and productivity.
I'm appearing for exams soon. Is it too late to start? +
Not at all. Even a few weeks of consistent practice can noticeably reduce anxiety, improve sleep quality, and sharpen focus before exams. Starting now is always better than not starting.
What exactly happens in a session? +
Each 35-minute session includes classical yogic practices — joint activation, breath work, specific postures, and short meditative techniques. All are gentle, simple, and done from wherever you are.
How do I pay? +
After registering, a Google Pay QR code will appear on screen. Pay via any UPI app — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc. UPI ID: shruti.shruti.jain84@okaxis. Batch confirmation is sent within 24 hours via WhatsApp or SMS.
Can I register with friends and get a discount? +
Yes! Register as a group of 2 for 10% off, 3 for 15% off, or 5+ for 25% off — each person pays the discounted rate. The fee calculator on the registration page works it out automatically.
