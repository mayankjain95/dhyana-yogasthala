Frequently asked questions

Who can attend?

Anyone aged 14 and above can attend.

I am an overthinker and have stress and anxiety. Will it help?

Yes, practices offered will bring mental clarity. You will see that gradually you become more focussed and more calm and peaceful within yourself.

I only want to focus on physical health. Will it help?

Yes, it will be supportive in improving physical health, mobility, flexibility, raising energy levels and immunity.

Do I need any prior yoga experience?

No prior experience is needed.

Do I need to be on an empty stomach?

Please keep a gap of four hours after a full meal before the class. You can have a light snack until 2.5 hours before the program starts. If you have any health concern about maintaining this gap, consult the teacher and she will guide you accordingly.

I am scared. I may get injured.

The practices are taught step by step, and the batch is small, so you receive personal attention. You don’t need to worry about getting it wrong.

I have a medical condition. Can I still join?

Please write to the teacher before registering, and consult your doctor as well.
